Privacy Policy for Screen Recorder: HD Video

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Screen Recorder: HD Video ("the App", "we", "our"), package name com.gprecorder.app, is a screen recording application for Android developed by Flindly. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user data.

Data Collection

The App itself does not require account creation, login, or personal identifiers, and does not use analytics or crash reporting SDKs. However, the App displays advertisements through Google AdMob, which may collect certain data as described below.

Screen Recordings and Storage

Screen recordings made using Screen Recorder: HD Video are captured and stored entirely on your device's local storage (e.g., DCIM, Movies, or a folder you select). These recordings are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with us or any external server. You have full control over these files and can delete them at any time through the App or your device's file manager.

Permissions

Screen Recorder: HD Video requests the following Android permissions solely to provide its core functionality:

  • Screen capture (MediaProjection): required to record your device screen.
  • Microphone (optional, if enabled): required only if you choose to record audio alongside your screen.
  • Storage access: required to save recordings to a location of your choice on your device.
  • Foreground service / notifications: required to keep the recording running reliably and show recording status.

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit your screen recordings off your device. Advertising data collection, described below, is handled separately by Google AdMob and is unrelated to your recordings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process certain data to serve ads, which can include:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (device type, operating system version)
  • General location information (such as country or region, derived from IP address)
  • App interaction data (such as ad impressions and clicks)

Depending on your device and region settings, ads shown may be personalized (based on your interests and activity) or non-personalized (based on general context only). You can review or adjust your ad personalization settings through your device's Settings > Google > Ads menu, or opt out of personalized advertising there.

Google's use of data collected through AdMob is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can also learn more about how Google uses data from partner sites and apps at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third-Party Services

Aside from Google AdMob for advertising, Screen Recorder: HD Video does not integrate any other third-party SDKs that collect or share user data (such as analytics SDKs). If this changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated in advance, and the update will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

Children's Privacy

Screen Recorder: HD Video is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can address it. Note that if you are a parent or guardian and believe your child's device has interacted with advertising in a manner inconsistent with applicable children's privacy laws, please contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, including to reflect changes in the App's advertising practices. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
